01 / WHY IT EXISTS

THE WORDS CAME
BEFORE THE PRODUCT

“Remember why you started all this.” is the thought behind the first TEKEA piece. It is a reminder to return to the reason you began when the work gets difficult, noisy or uncertain.

The hoodie turns that thought into something physical. DROP 001 marks the point where TEKEA stopped existing only on screens and became something you can wear.
